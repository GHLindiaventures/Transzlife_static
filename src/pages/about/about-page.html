Chapter 01Roots

Rooted in 5,000 years of Ayurvedic wisdom

For thousands of years, classical texts such as the Charaka Samhita and Sushruta Samhita have documented plants that support health and vitality.

But raw herbs are hard to get right: active levels vary, the taste is harsh, and much of their potency is lost in crude powders. TranzLife was founded to bridge that gap, starting with quality botanicals that are checked for purity before they enter our formulation process.

  • Natural ingredients
  • Ayurvedic wisdom
  • 100% plant-derived
  • Science backed

Chapter 02Science

Where modern science unlocks bioavailability

We don't simply grind herbs into capsules. We extract, standardise and protect the active plant compounds that make each formula work.
